The idea

A three-bedroom house placed along a single contour so every room faces the Himalayan skyline without terracing the hillside.

The setting

An east-facing ridge above Mukteshwar, surrounded by old pine and a small existing apple orchard.

The residence

Stone base, timber upper level, deep eaves and a long verandah designed for cold-weather sun.

The experience

Slow mornings above the cloud line, walking distance to the temple ridge, and a village market twenty minutes away.

The community

Nine independent plots with shared access road, water infrastructure and estate maintenance.
