Confidence

Buy with
confidence.

We do not publish inventory. Every project passes through a review before it reaches you and an advisor stays with you afterwards.

  1. Project Verified

    We visit the site and confirm the project as presented.

  2. Documents Reviewed

    Available project documentation is read by our advisory team.

  3. Land / Title Checks

    Ownership structure and land records are examined before listing.

  4. Curated for Quality

    Siting, construction and liveability are assessed on our criteria.

  5. Advisor Support

    A specialist stays with you from first question to possession.
