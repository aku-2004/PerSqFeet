About

We believe
where you live
changes how you live.

Why we exist

The second-home market is fragmented.

Inventory is scattered across brokers, developers and word of mouth. Quality is inconsistent, information is thin, and the buyer is usually left to verify everything alone. We built a narrower, better-informed path.

Our approach

Curated, verified, advisory-led.

We review far more projects than we present. What reaches you has been visited, read and assessed.

Our network

Developers, advisors, partners.

Long-standing relationships with regional developers, legal advisors, architects, hospitality operators and property managers across India.

How we work

Discovery to ownership.

Discovery → Advisory → Viewing → Negotiation → Purchase → Ownership.
One relationship across the whole arc, not a handoff at the point of sale.

Our belief

Life, not square footage.

A second home is measured in weekends, gatherings and quiet mornings. We help you look beyond the property to the life it can offer, while bringing the same care to value, documentation and long term ownership.

A different way to own a second home.
